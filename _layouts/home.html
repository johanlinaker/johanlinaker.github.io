Hi there! I'm a Senior Researcher at RISE Research Institutes of Sweden and an Adjunct Assistant Professor at Lund University, based in Malmö, Sweden.

My research looks at how open source software, open source AI and data sharing can help governments and industry strengthen digital sovereignty, interoperability and open innovation. I work closely with public-sector organisations, companies and policymakers, from national networks in Sweden to EU and UN initiatives, to turn research into practical strategies, guidelines and communities.

Here you'll find my publications, talks and other activities, along with posts on current developments. For collaborations, talks or interviews, get in touch.

Notes, thoughts and summaries of talks, papers and reports. Some in English, some in Swedish, depending on the context :)
